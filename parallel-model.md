# The parallel model

There is an economy outside our door and we have no argument with it. It is
how the beer arrives, how the rent is paid, how the electrician is thanked for
coming out on a Sunday. We use it every day and we intend to keep using it.

Inside, we run something else. Not against it. Alongside it, touching it at one
place and nowhere else.

Here is the whole of it: **nobody buys anything, and nobody sells anything.**

---

## A needle, not a number

Picture a dial with a needle, and the needle can travel from left to right.

When you join, your needle sits at the far left. In that position you can take
nothing from the association. This is not a punishment and it is not a debt. It
is simply where a person stands who has not yet given anything — the honest
starting place, the same one everybody has had.

Give something to the association and the needle moves right.

Take something from the association and it moves back left.

That is the entire mechanism. Everything else in this document is a
consequence of it.

The people whose needles sit furthest right are the people who have given the
most and taken the least, and they can take more than the rest of us, and at
the end of each month the association makes something of that. Not because
they paid for the privilege. Because that is what the dial is showing.

---

## Why it is a dial and not a currency

We could have called them points and given you a balance. We deliberately did
not, and the difference is not cosmetic.

**A dial has no denomination.** You cannot hold seven of a dial. You cannot
send someone half your dial, or keep it under your mattress, or sell it to a
person who wants a shortcut. There is nothing to hold. There is only where your
needle happens to be pointing today, and it points there because of things you
did, and it will point somewhere else after you do other things.

A balance is a thing you own. A position is a description of you.

You cannot buy a position, because nothing is on sale. Nobody has priced it,
nobody is obliged to hand it over, and no amount of anything entitles you to
demand it. It moves when you do something for the association, and giving money
is one of the things a person can do — a common one, an honourable one, and not
a special one. A sofa moves it. Three hours behind the decks on a Friday moves
it. Photographing the courtyard so the website stops looking empty moves it.
Carrying chairs after an event that ran too long moves it.

We know the objection, because it is the first one we made ourselves: if
handing over money moves the needle right, and a needle further right lets you
take a drink, then have you not just bought a drink with extra steps?

Our answer is that a purchase is a promise, and there is no promise here.
Nothing you give creates an entitlement to anything. You can give for a year
and take nothing and that is a perfectly ordinary way to belong to an
association — most members of most associations do exactly that. You cannot
ask for it back. You cannot convert it into anything. When you leave, you take
nothing with you, and nobody has ever suggested otherwise. What the needle
records is not what you are owed. It is what you have done.

If that distinction is too fine to survive, we would rather find out from
somebody who argues it well than discover it later on our own.

---

## Both directions matter

Most schemes of this kind only count upward. You collect, you accumulate, the
number never falls, and after a while the number is the point and the thing it
was supposed to describe has been forgotten.

Ours falls. Take a drink and the needle moves left, the same as it moved right
when you gave. Not as a penalty — as an accurate description. The association
gave you something; you have less standing than you did an hour ago; the dial
should say so. A record that only goes up is a record that has stopped telling
the truth.

This also means nobody sits at the right-hand end forever. Someone who gives a
great deal and takes a great deal ends up in the middle, which is exactly
right, and it means the top of the dial belongs to whoever is currently holding
the place up rather than to whoever held it up in 2024.

---

## Where the two models touch

The association lives in the ordinary economy and pays its way in it. Invoices,
suppliers, taxes, a landlord, an accountant. Euros go out constantly.

Nothing comes back the other way. We do not sell drinks, we do not sell
entrance, we do not sell membership. The association is funded because the
people in it decide to fund it, and that funding is not a transaction with a
counterparty and a price. It is what a members' association is.

So the two systems meet at a single boundary, and at that boundary money only
ever flows one way — out into the world, to people who quite reasonably want
paying. Inside the walls there is no market at all. There is a room, some
people who look after it, and a dial for each of them.

We think this is why the parallel model does not have to fight the mainstream
one. It is not trying to replace anything. It has no ambitions about currency,
no token, nothing to speculate on, nothing to list anywhere. It is a way of
keeping track of who is holding the roof up, and it only works at the scale of
a place where people know each other by sight.

---

## What we are careful about

Three things we watch, and will keep watching in the open.

**Anyone who can move a needle can move it wrongly.** Deciding what a sofa is
worth is a judgement, and judgement is exactly what cannot be automated here.
So every movement is recorded — who decided, for whom, for what, in plain
words — and only the association's officers may decide, and their decisions can
be looked at afterwards by anybody with the standing to look.

**A ranking of givers can turn into a competition nobody meant to enter.** We
built the top of the dial to recognise generosity. It could just as easily
reward compulsion, and a private club with a bar is not the place to be casual
about that. Members appear on it under three-word pseudonyms rather than their
names, staff cannot see who is who, and if the ranking ever starts to feel like
a scoreboard rather than a thank-you, it should go.

**Recognising work with a needle is close to paying for work.** A DJ who plays
for three hours has done something real, and the association wants to
acknowledge it. We are getting proper advice about where acknowledgement ends
and employment begins, because wanting a thing to be true has never once made
it true.

---

## Where this is

It is being built. The association exists, the room exists, the people exist;
the dial is under construction and the euro-denominated version it replaces
has never held anybody's money but the owner's, in testing.

That is a good moment to publish a design rather than a result. Nothing has
calcified. If you can see the flaw in this, now is when telling us costs us
almost nothing to act on.
